# Signal Taxonomy

Use this taxonomy to classify account news for Slack sales actions. Assign one primary signal and optional secondary signals.

## Positive Opportunity Signals

### Growth and budget capacity

- Revenue growth, profitability improvement, large new contracts, market share gains
- Funding, pre-IPO activity, IPO preparation, M&A-driven growth
- Hiring growth, new offices, global expansion, business unit expansion
- New product, service category, marketplace, partner, or ecosystem expansion

Recommended action: explore collaboration scale, cross-functional workflows, external collaboration, security, compliance, and Enterprise Grid or Enterprise+ fit.

### Digital workplace momentum

- Google Workspace, Salesforce, Atlassian, ServiceNow, Okta, AWS, cloud, AI, DX, AX, data platform, or workflow automation adoption
- Company-wide productivity, collaboration, knowledge management, or AI transformation initiatives
- New CIO, CTO, CDO, COO, CHRO, or transformation leader with explicit modernization agenda

Recommended action: position Slack as the conversational layer for enterprise workflow, AI adoption, automation, and cross-tool collaboration.

### New-logo or greenfield signal

- Fast-growing company with no known Slack usage
- Distributed teams, partner-heavy operations, developer or product-led organization
- Recent tooling modernization without an announced work hub

Recommended action: validate collaboration stack and identify executive sponsor, IT owner, and business workflow owner.

## Negative Risk Signals

### Direct Slack attrition risk

- Slack cancellation, suspension, migration away from Slack, competitor replacement, or public criticism of Slack usage
- Tool consolidation that explicitly targets collaboration or messaging spend

Recommended action: treat as severe. Identify account owner, renewal date, executive sponsor, usage health, and immediate recovery plan.

### Budget or organizational pressure

- Layoffs, restructuring, hiring freeze, major cost reduction, liquidity issues, delayed IPO, declining revenue, regulatory fines, or operational crisis
- M&A integration where duplicate tooling may be rationalized

Recommended action: review plan fit, renewal exposure, active use cases, executive value proof, and consolidation narrative.

### Security, compliance, and trust pressure

- Data breach, regulatory investigation, internal control issues, public compliance failure, sector-specific security pressure

Recommended action: lead with Enterprise security, governance, retention, eDiscovery, DLP, compliance, and admin controls when relevant.

## Neutral or Monitor Signals

- Brand campaign, minor product updates, awards, executive interviews without operational change
- News unrelated to workforce, technology, growth, budget, risk, or collaboration

Recommended action: monitor only unless combined with stronger account context.

## Confidence

- High: multiple reputable sources or one primary source directly supports the signal.
- Medium: one reputable source supports the signal, but Slack implication is inferred.
- Low: source is weak, old, duplicated, vague, or only indirectly connected.
