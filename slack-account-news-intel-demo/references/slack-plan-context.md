# Slack Plan Context

Use this reference to interpret account plans and avoid incorrect upsell language.

## Current Plan Context

Official Slack plan comparisons currently list Free, Pro, Business+, and Enterprise+ plans. Slack announced plan changes in June 2025, including expanded AI, security, and Salesforce features, the retirement of the Slack AI add-on for website purchases, and the introduction of Enterprise+.

## Commercial Motion Rules

### Free or Pro

Possible motions:

- Upgrade to Business+ for advanced AI, Salesforce, analytics, admin, and security needs
- Upgrade to Enterprise+ for large, regulated, multi-entity, compliance-heavy, or enterprise-search needs
- Convert active teams from lightweight collaboration to governed company-wide collaboration

### Business+

Possible motions:

- Upgrade to Enterprise+ when the account needs enterprise search, legal holds, Discovery API, audit logs, HIPAA support, domain claiming, custom roles, stronger governance, or multi-workspace enterprise administration
- Expand paid users, departments, workflows, Slack Connect, automation, and Salesforce workflows

### Enterprise, Enterprise Grid, or Enterprise+

Do not describe the opportunity as simple plan-tier upsell. These accounts are already at or near the top of the Slack plan structure.

Use these motions instead:

- Seat expansion: more paid users, more departments, more frontline or regional teams
- Workspace or org expansion: additional workspaces, subsidiaries, acquired companies, overseas entities, or partner teams
- Use-case expansion: incident response, customer support, sales, engineering, product, HR, security, compliance, partner collaboration
- Slack Connect expansion: external partners, customers, suppliers, agencies, hospitals, clinics, franchisees, distributors
- AI and Salesforce workflow adoption: summaries, huddle notes, search, recaps, workflow automation, Salesforce channels, Sales Home, Agentforce-aligned workflows
- Governance and renewal defense: audit logs, legal holds, Discovery API, retention, eDiscovery, DLP, admin controls, compliance
- Value protection: show business-critical workflows before renewal or procurement review

## Language Guidance

Use "확장 기회" instead of "플랜 업셀" for Enterprise accounts.

Good:

- "사용 인원 및 워크스페이스 확장 기회"
- "Slack Connect 기반 외부 협업 확장"
- "AI/세일즈포스 워크플로우 활용 확대"
- "갱신 방어 및 가치 증명 필요"

Avoid:

- "Enterprise 고객에게 상위 플랜 업그레이드 제안"
- "이미 Enterprise인데 Enterprise로 업셀"
- "뉴스가 긍정적이므로 무조건 예산 증가"
